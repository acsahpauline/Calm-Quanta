CalmQuanta - AI-Powered Mindfulness App:
CalmQuanta is an innovative AI-powered mindfulness app designed to enhance mental well-being by offering personalized insights and interactive features. It leverages machine learning models and data analysis to track emotional patterns, suggest self-care activities, and help users manage stress, anxiety, and other emotional states. With a variety of features like mood tracking, breathing exercises, ambient soundscapes, journaling, daily quotes, and more, CalmQuanta provides a holistic approach to improving mental health.

Features:
1. Mood Tracking: Allows users to select their current mood and track it over time, offering insights into emotional patterns.
2. Breathing Exercises: Provides interactive breathing exercises to help reduce stress and anxiety.
3. Ambient Sounds: Offers a variety of soothing ambient soundscapes, such as birds chirping, ocean waves, gentle rain, and more, to help users relax.
4. Mindful Journaling: Encourages users to write their thoughts and reflect on their feelings in a safe, private space.
5. Daily Quotes: Motivational and inspiring quotes to uplift your mood every day.
6. AI Suggestions: Personalized suggestions based on your emotional state, helping you manage your mood and mental well-being.
7. Weekly Mood Reports: Summarizes your mood trends for the week, providing insights into emotional progress.
8. Guided Meditation: Relax and rejuvenate with guided meditation sessions that promote mindfulness and focus.
9. VR Forest Mode: Immerse yourself in a calming virtual forest to meditate and relax.

Technologies Used:
1. Streamlit: For the app’s user interface and deployment.
2. Python: For the core functionality and logic.
3. Pandas & Numpy: For data manipulation and analysis.
4. Base64: For embedding background images.
5. MP3 Audio Files: For ambient sounds and audio effects.
